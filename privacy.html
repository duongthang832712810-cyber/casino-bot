Short Summary

Read This First

LuckyBot+ stores a limited set of Discord identifiers and virtual economy/game data necessary to operate balances, daily rewards, profiles, achievements, leaderboards, Blackjack, Coin Flip, Lottery, Sicbo, Baucua, Lucky Mining, announcements, and server configuration features. No real personal information beyond Discord identifiers needed by the bot is intentionally collected. We do not sell, rent, share, or commercialize your data in any form. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting the bot owner.

02

Information We Do Not Collect

Boundaries

To be explicit about what we do not collect:

  • Passwords, authentication tokens, or account credentials of any kind.
  • Payment information, financial data, bank account numbers, or credit card details.
  • Government-issued identification, real names, physical addresses, or personally identifiable information (PII) beyond Discord's internal numeric IDs.
  • Email addresses — you are never required to provide an email to use this bot.
  • General message content, private messages, or the contents of Discord channels. For prefix commands, the bot may receive and process the command message text needed to execute that command, but it does not intentionally store general chat logs.
  • Device information, IP addresses, browser fingerprints, or geolocation data.
  • Real money, cryptocurrency, or any financial transaction data.
  • Information about users who never interact directly with the bot via commands.
// Data Usage & Storage
03

How We Use Information

Purpose

All collected data is used exclusively for operating the bot's features. Specifically, stored data is used to:

  • Create and maintain individual player profiles, virtual balances, experience points, levels, achievements, current ticket counts, and statistics.
  • Process economy commands such as balance/profile views, daily rewards, virtual coin transfers, and leaderboard ranking.
  • Start, save, resume, and resolve Blackjack, Coin Flip, Lottery, Sicbo, and Baucua activity across bot restarts where applicable.
  • Enforce one-active-game-per-user rules for active single-player games and prevent duplicate unresolved sessions.
  • Subtract bets or purchases, calculate payouts, update virtual balances, update EXP/stats, and record per-game results atomically.
  • Manage and distribute lottery tickets, process scheduled lottery draws, update jackpot pools, pay winners, and clear per-draw ticket records after each draw.
  • Track Lucky Mining computer ownership, same-tier purchase counts, last claim timestamps, deterministic virtual coin generation, and claim cooldowns.
  • Send, edit, delete, and recover Discord embed messages and game result announcements in configured channels.
  • Maintain server-specific configuration such as designated Lottery, Sicbo, and Baucua announcement channels.
  • Respond to support and abuse inquiries, investigate reported bugs, diagnose operational errors, and restore service after restart or Discord API failures.
  • Detect and prevent abuse, exploitation, cheating, automation, command spam, or violations of these Terms.

Data is never used for advertising, profiling, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to operating the bot.

04

Data Storage & Security

Infrastructure

Bot data is stored in a SQLite database controlled and maintained by the bot owner. The bot uses this database as the source of truth for balances, active games, global rounds, tickets, mining computers, achievements, and configuration. The following technical measures are applied to protect stored data:

  • Access to the database is restricted to authorized bot processes and the bot owner only.
  • Bot tokens, environment variables, database paths, and operational secrets are stored outside public user-facing pages and should never be exposed publicly.
  • The server and database software are kept up to date to mitigate known security vulnerabilities.
  • Operational logs containing sensitive debug information are retained for a limited period and are not publicly accessible.

However, no system connected to the internet can guarantee absolute security. While we apply reasonable safeguards, we cannot warrant that stored data will be completely immune to unauthorized access, data breaches, or unforeseen security events. In the event of a significant data breach affecting user data, we will make reasonable efforts to notify affected users through available channels.

05

Data Sharing

Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, lease, or otherwise transfer your data to third parties for any commercial purpose. Data may only be shared in the following limited and necessary circumstances:

  • Hosting providers: The database and bot process run on infrastructure provided by a hosting service. This provider may have incidental access to stored data as part of their infrastructure operation, governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Security or abuse response: If we believe your account is involved in a security incident, attack, or serious abuse of the bot, relevant data may be reviewed or shared as part of the investigation.
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose data if required by a valid legal process, court order, law enforcement request, or other binding legal obligation.
  • Discord platform: The bot operates on Discord's platform, and your use of Discord is additionally governed by Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Beyond the above, no data will be shared with any third party without your explicit consent.

06

Data Retention

Lifecycle

// Persistent Data

  • User profile rows, coin balance, EXP, level, daily reward timestamp, aggregate stats, per-game stats, achievement unlocks, Lucky Mining computers, and mining stats are retained while the bot continues to provide services and the user's data has not been requested for deletion.
  • Server configuration data, including announcement channel and message IDs for Lottery, Sicbo, and Baucua, is retained for as long as the bot remains active in the server or until an administrator changes/removes the configuration.
  • Leaderboard data is derived from persistent profile, stats, achievement, and economy rows.

// Temporary / Cyclical Data

  • Blackjack active game rows are deleted when the game resolves, is cleared, or is otherwise ended by service logic.
  • Coin Flip pending rows are deleted after the delayed result resolves and the payout/stat update is completed.
  • Sicbo and Baucua round/bet data is retained for the active or current round as needed for betting, display, payout, and result resolution.
  • Lottery ticket records are cleared after each lottery draw cycle completes; the lightweight Lottery state keeps only current draw state and limited last-draw summary fields.
  • Channel/message references for announcements may be replaced when the bot sends a new announcement message.
  • Operational logs and error traces are retained for a limited time (typically 30–90 days) for debugging and security review, then purged.

// Extended Retention

  • Some data may be retained beyond standard periods if necessary to investigate active abuse cases, recover from data corruption, resolve ongoing disputes, reverse exploit gains, or comply with applicable legal obligations.
  • Backups, if maintained, may retain older copies of data for a limited period before rotation. Deletion from backups may occur on the normal backup lifecycle rather than instantly.
07

Your Rights & Data Control

User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. We respect these rights and will honor valid requests to the best of our ability:

  • Right to access: You can request a summary of bot database data associated with your Discord User ID, including profile, stats, achievements, tickets, active game rows, and mining data where applicable.
  • Right to deletion: You can request that your bot-related data (profile, balance, achievements, game history, tickets, mining computers, stats, and active states) be deleted by contacting the bot owner. Note that some data may be temporarily retained if needed for active security investigations, abuse review, ongoing game resolution, dispute handling, or legal compliance.
  • Right to correction: If you believe data associated with your account is inaccurate because of a bug, outage, or command error, you may request a review. Virtual balances and game outcomes are not guaranteed to be changed unless the bot owner determines correction is appropriate.
  • Right to restriction: You may request that we stop processing your data in certain ways while a deletion or correction request is pending, subject to technical feasibility and abuse-prevention needs.

Server owners may additionally request removal of their server's configuration and guild-level data by contacting the bot owner and confirming their ownership of the relevant server.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact the bot owner using the contact information provided in Section 12. We aim to respond to all valid requests within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Protection

LuckyBot+ is not directed at or designed for children who are not permitted to use Discord under Discord's Terms of Service for their region. We do not knowingly collect, store, or process personal information from users who do not meet Discord's minimum age requirement.

If a parent or guardian believes their child has interacted with the bot in violation of Discord's age requirements, they should contact the bot owner immediately. We will take reasonable steps to delete any data associated with the user's account upon confirmation.

Server administrators have a particular responsibility to ensure the bot is used appropriately within their community, including enforcing age-appropriate access.

09

Security Practices

Security

We take the security of stored data seriously and implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Specific measures include:

  • Database access is restricted exclusively to the bot process and the bot owner — no public database endpoints are exposed.
  • All credentials, tokens, and API keys are stored as environment variables and are never committed to any public code repository.
  • The bot host and database server software are regularly updated to apply security patches.
  • Sensitive operational logs are stored separately from economy data and have restricted access.

Despite these measures, no internet-connected system can provide absolute security guarantees. You use this bot understanding that residual security risks exist, as with any online service.
